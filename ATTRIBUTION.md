# Attribution

## LIBERO

The annotated episodes are demonstrations from the LIBERO benchmark
(LIBERO-Object and LIBERO-10 task suites).

> B. Liu, Y. Zhu, C. Gao, Y. Feng, Q. Liu, Y. Zhu, P. Stone,
> "LIBERO: Benchmarking Knowledge Transfer for Lifelong Robot Learning,"
> NeurIPS 2023 Datasets and Benchmarks Track.
> https://libero-project.github.io/

LIBERO's code is licensed MIT; its datasets are licensed CC BY 4.0.

## LeRobot conversion

Episode observations were read from the HuggingFace LeRobot conversion of
LIBERO, `HuggingFaceVLA/libero`. The `episode_index` values used throughout
this repository refer to that conversion.

> LeRobot: https://github.com/huggingface/lerobot
